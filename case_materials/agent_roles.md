# Agent roles for the classroom simulation

These bullet points are **mirrored** in `seed_un_ai_climate_governance.txt` under **Stakeholder briefings** (what the LLM sees). If you change roles for class, update **both** so handouts and simulation stay aligned.

## United States

### Interests:
- support innovation and private sector participation
- avoid unlimited financial obligation
- promote transparency and auditability
- protect national security and intellectual property

### Likely position:
Supports a fund if governance is accountable and private firms can participate.

## European Union

### Interests:
- human rights, transparency, regulation, safeguards
- climate finance and multilateral cooperation
- data protection and trustworthy AI

### Likely position:
Supports a fund with strong governance, auditability, and rights-based safeguards.

## China

### Interests:
- technology cooperation and infrastructure development
- respect for national sovereignty
- opposition to externally imposed monitoring
- recognition of development rights

### Likely position:
Supports capacity building and technology transfer, but resists governance perceived as external control.

## Climate Vulnerable States Coalition

### Interests:
- urgent adaptation finance
- affordable technology access
- local capacity building
- loss and damage recognition
- protection from data extraction

### Likely position:
Demands stronger commitments from high income and high capacity actors.

## Civil Society and Digital Rights Coalition

### Interests:
- public oversight
- anti-surveillance safeguards
- inclusion of local communities
- open accountability mechanisms
- protection against dependency on foreign technology vendors

### Likely position:
Supports the fund only if safeguards and participation are mandatory.
